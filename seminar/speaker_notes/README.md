# seminar/speaker_notes/

Phase **S7**. Empty — written after the slides exist (S2–S6).

One file per act: `act_I.md` … `act_X.md`.

## What goes in a speaker note

- The **question** this slide answers (it is already in `source_map.md` — keep them consistent).
- The transition *in* and the transition *out*. Transitions are what make a talk feel like a story
  rather than a list.
- The **one sentence** that must be said on this slide.
- Anything that must **not** be claimed — especially where a result is provisional, simulated
  rather than measured, or unverified.
- A timing mark.

## Why they exist

Mostly so that under the pressure of a live talk, no unsupported claim gets made by accident.
A slide can be honest while the words over it are not. The notes are where "this is simulated,
not measured" is written down so it actually gets said.
