# DECISION TEMPLATE

Append to `planning/decision_log.md`.

> **A decision is the only thing that promotes something to CONFIRMED.** Conversation does not.
> An AI suggestion does not. A plausible default does not.

---

```markdown
### D-0NN — <short title>

**Status:** CONFIRMED | PROVISIONAL | OPEN | BLOCKED | SUPERSEDED
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Decided by:** <student / student+Artan / forced by constraint>
**Gate:** <which gate this serves>

**Decision.**
<What was decided. One or two sentences. Specific enough to be checkable.>

**Why.**
<The reasoning. Cite claim IDs from research/claims_and_sources.md or source IDs
from the bibliography where the reasoning rests on a reference. If it rests on a
lab fact, cite the row in design/hardware_constraints.md.>

**Alternatives considered.**
<What else was on the table, and why each lost. A decision with no rejected
alternative was not a decision.>

**Rules out.**
<What this closes off. Being explicit here is what stops the same question being
reopened in three weeks.>

**Would reverse it.**
<The specific evidence that would change this. "New information" is not an answer.
If nothing could reverse it, say so.>

**Consequences.**
<What now has to change: files to update, blocked items that unblock.>
```

---

## Rules

1. **Date and attribute every entry.** A decision with no date cannot be ordered against the
   others, and a project's reasoning only makes sense in order.
2. **A mentor's answer is recorded the same day**, in his words where it matters.
3. **Never delete.** Mark SUPERSEDED and write the new entry. The reasoning chain is more valuable
   than the current answer — a future session needs to know what was already tried.
4. **"Would reverse it" is mandatory.** A decision you cannot imagine reversing is usually an
   assumption in disguise.
5. **PROVISIONAL is honest and CONFIRMED is a commitment.** When in doubt, PROVISIONAL. The whole
   point of the status vocabulary is that a provisional choice does not quietly harden.
